# Cover Letter Skill

## Purpose
Generate concise, professional cover letters for finance and real estate roles,
tailored to the job description and the candidate's background.

## Inputs to extract before writing
1. **Job title and company name** — from the posting or user message
2. **Key requirements** — 2–3 skills or experiences the role emphasizes
3. **Candidate differentiators** — match against requirements; pull from CV or context

## Structure (always follow this)

**Opening line**: State the role and one specific reason this company/role is a fit.
Not generic ("I am excited to apply") — reference something concrete about the firm.

**Paragraph 1 – Match**: Connect 2 of the job's key requirements to the
candidate's most relevant experience. Be specific (deal count, geography, asset class).

**Paragraph 2 – Value-add**: One differentiator that goes beyond the JD
(language skills, technical tools, cross-border deal experience, team leadership).

**Closing**: Brief, confident. No "thank you for your consideration" filler.
Offer availability for a call. Sign off formally.

## Tone
- Confident, not boastful
- Specific over generic — numbers and named markets beat adjectives
- British English spelling for London roles; standard for others

## Length
- Target: 220–280 words
- Hard max: 320 words

## Output format
Return the letter as plain text, ready to copy. No commentary before or after
unless the user asks for it. If key inputs are missing (company name, role title),
ask for them before writing — do not invent them.
